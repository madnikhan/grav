## 🖼️ **Adding Your Hero Image**

### **File Location:**
```

```

### **Image Requirements:**
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Dimensions**: 600x800px or similar aspect ratio
- **Content**: Model applying or holding skincare products
- **Style**: Professional, clean, beauty-focused
- **Brand**: Should represent Silbele's luxury aesthetic

### **Image Suggestions:**
1. **Model applying cream** to face
2. **Model holding skincare products** elegantly
3. **Close-up of hands** with beauty products
4. **Lifestyle shot** with skincare routine
5. **Professional beauty shot** with products

### **Technical Specifications:**
- **File size**: Under 500KB for fast loading
- **Resolution**: High quality (300 DPI)
- **Background**: Clean, neutral, or blurred
- **Lighting**: Soft, professional beauty lighting
- **Colors**: Should complement your brand palette
