<div class="faq-header">
<h2>Body Care FAQs</h2>
<p>Get answers to common questions about body care and skincare for your body.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-grid">
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How often should I exfoliate my body?</h3>
<p>For most skin types, exfoliate 2-3 times per week. If you have sensitive skin, start with once per week and gradually increase frequency.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>Can I use body care products on my face?</h3>
<p>No, body care products are formulated specifically for body skin, which is thicker than facial skin. Always use facial products for your face.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>What's the difference between body and facial skincare?</h3>
<p>Body skin is thicker and less sensitive than facial skin, so body products can contain higher concentrations of active ingredients.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How do I treat body acne?</h3>
<p>Use gentle exfoliation and targeted treatments like our Glycolic Acid Exfoliating Body Stick. Avoid harsh scrubs that can irritate the skin.</p>
</div>
