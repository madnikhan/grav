<div class="routine-faq-section">
<div class="faq-header">
<h2>Routine Building FAQs</h2>
<p>Get answers to common questions about building your perfect skincare routine.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-grid">
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How many products should I include in my routine?</h3>
<p>Start with 3-4 essential products: cleanser, serum, moisturiser, and SPF. You can add more products as your skin gets used to the routine.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>What order should I apply my products?</h3>
<p>Apply products from thinnest to thickest: cleanser → serum → moisturiser → SPF. This ensures optimal absorption and effectiveness.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>Can I use multiple serums together?</h3>
<p>Yes, but be careful with active ingredients. Avoid using multiple exfoliants or strong actives together. Layer from thinnest to thickest consistency.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How long should I wait between products?</h3>
<p>Wait 1-2 minutes between products to allow proper absorption. For active ingredients like retinol, wait 5-10 minutes before applying the next product.</p>
</div>
