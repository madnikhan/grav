<div class="faq-header">
<h2>Hair Care FAQs</h2>
<p>Our hair care experts are here to answer your questions and help you find the perfect products for your hair type and concerns.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-grid">
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How do I choose the right hair care products?</h3>
<p>Start by identifying your hair type and main concerns. Consider factors like hair texture, scalp condition, and styling needs. Our Haircare Quiz can help you find the perfect products for your specific needs.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How often should I wash my hair?</h3>
<p>This depends on your hair type and scalp condition. Oily scalps may need daily washing, while dry hair might only need washing 2-3 times per week. Listen to your hair and adjust accordingly.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>Can I use multiple hair care products together?</h3>
<p>Yes, but it's important to layer them correctly. Start with scalp treatments, then apply styling products. Avoid overloading your hair with too many products at once.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<h3>How long does it take to see results from hair care products?</h3>
<p>Most hair care products show immediate results for styling and frizz control. For hair growth and scalp health, you may need 4-8 weeks to see significant improvements.</p>
</div>
